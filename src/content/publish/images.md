Every plugin has an icon. A banner and a gallery are optional. The hub re-encodes every image before anyone sees it, so it never serves your
files as uploaded.

| Image | Required | Format | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Icon | Yes | PNG or WebP, square, transparency allowed | 256 to 1024 pixels, at most 1 MB |
| Banner | No | PNG, WebP or JPEG, 2:1 | 1280 by 640 to 2560 by 1280, at most 2 MB |
| Gallery | No, up to 6 | PNG, WebP or JPEG, 16:10 recommended | At most 2560 by 1600, at most 2 MB each |

## Rules

- SVG and animated images are not accepted.
- Do not imitate Runesmith's logo, the hub's badges or another plugin's icon. An administrator looks at your icon and banner when you
  register and whenever they change.
- Keep text out of icons; it is unreadable at small sizes.

## Where they appear

The icon appears everywhere: search results, your plugin page, the plugin manager in Runesmith and the install dialog. The banner tops your
plugin page and can appear in the featured row on the home page. Gallery images appear above your README.
