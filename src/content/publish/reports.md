Users can report a plugin from its page here and in Runesmith.

| Report | Where it goes | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broken or doesn't work | An issue in your repository | Answer and fix it as you would any issue. If a problem that breaks Runesmith or loses data goes unanswered for 14 days, users can escalate it to the registry |
| Breaks the rules | A public issue in the registry | An administrator asks you to fix it within 14 days. Impersonation is acted on immediately |
| Security | A private report to Runesmith | An administrator contacts you privately |

## Security reports

1. Answer within 14 days. Unanswered security reports can cost a plugin its verified status.
2. Work with the administrator privately on a fix. While it is investigated, new installs may be paused; existing users keep the plugin.
3. Release the fixed version. The administrator then lifts the pause, or blocks affected versions and publishes an advisory.

## If your account is compromised

Tell Runesmith at once through the [security page](/security). An administrator can pause installs of your plugin within minutes while you
recover your account.

## Appeals

If you disagree with a moderation decision, open an **Appeal** issue in the
[registry](https://github.com/RunesmithHub/registry/issues/new/choose). A different administrator answers within seven days.
