This policy covers what users see of your plugin: its name, ID, icon, banner, screenshots, summary, README, release notes and keywords.

## Accurate

- Names, summaries and screenshots describe what the plugin does.
- Keywords and categories match the plugin. Do not stuff keywords.
- Release notes describe the changes in that version.

## Not misleading

- Do not impersonate Runesmith, the hub, another publisher or another plugin. This includes names that differ only by look-alike characters
  and names that add "official", "pro" or "plus" to an existing plugin's name.
- Do not use Runesmith's logo or the hub's badges in your icon, banner or screenshots.
- Do not claim a tier, a review or an endorsement you do not have.

## Respectful

- No offensive, hateful or sexual content.
- No content that infringes someone else's trademark or copyright.

## Enforcement

Administrators look at names, icons and banners when you register and whenever they change. Content that breaks this policy is reported
through the registry. You get 14 days to fix it; impersonation is acted on immediately.
